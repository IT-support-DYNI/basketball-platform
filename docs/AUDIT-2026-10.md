## 3. Page-by-Page Review

**A. Purpose:** a router. Signed-out visitors go to `/club`; signed-in users go to their dashboard (`app/page.tsx`). **B–I:** no UI. Returns 200 with a "Loading page…" shell (13.5 KB) before redirecting. **SEO:** the domain root has no `<h1>`, a generic title ("DYNI Blazers"), no canonical, and redirects with a **307 (temporary)** to `/club`, so search engines may index `/` and `/club` as separate pages. **J. ### PAGE: `/club` (landing)
**A. Purpose:** the club's front door. Explains the club and drives registration (primary CTA "Start registration").
**B. Visual design:** strong, consistent brand: dark editorial layout, condensed display type, one flame accent (LIVE screenshot). Problems:

**C. UX:** purpose and primary CTA are clear. The "Club news" section says "Nothing posted yet" on the homepage, which signals an unfinished site. The trials form ("Player name / Guardian email → Start registration") hands off to `/register`. Good.
**D. Functionality:**

**G. Accessibility:**
- **WCAG 2.2.2 Pause, Stop, Hide (A) — FAIL.** The hero carousel auto-rotates and the marquee ticker (`.ticker`, animation `dyni-marquee`) moves continuously for more than 5 s, with no pause/stop control. Reduced motion is respected only by `StatCount`.

- No skip link. Landmarks exist (`header`/`nav`×3/`main`/`footer`), so **2.4.1 is met via landmarks**, but a skip link is recommended.
- Contrast: automated sweep of visible text in dark theme found no real failures (flagged items were gradient-background false positives; the flame CTA on near-black ≈ 5.3:1).
**H. Performance:** 48.8 KB HTML, about 1.9 s TTFB on first (cold) request (LIVE `curl`). Self-hosted, preloaded fonts (7 woff2 files). Animations run on load.

**J. Security:** CSP, HSTS, XFO present (§8). PASS.

| Check | Result |

Two layers: middleware is a UX redirect only (`middleware.ts:1-9` says so explicitly), and each handler calls `requireAuth/requireRole/requireAbility/requirePlayerAccess`. Errors: one wrapper (`lib/api/route.ts`) maps unknown errors to a generic 500 with a request id (no stack traces leak). CORS: no custom CORS (same-origin only). PASS.

## 7. Database & Data Security

- **Passwords:** bcrypt cost 12 (`lib/password.ts`). PASS.
- **Admin/coach-provisioned temp passwords:** `word-NNNN-word` from 16 words × 9000 × 16 ≈ 2.3 M combinations (~21 bits) (`lib/password.ts:11`). `mustChangePassword` is enforced, but the temp password is shown in the API response/UI and probably relayed by phone/message. A per-email lockout makes online guessing slow. LOW.
- **Tokens:** auth and verification tokens hashed (SHA-256). Calendar tokens stored raw (acceptable for a feed secret, but they leak via SEC-01). TOTP secrets plaintext (LOW–MEDIUM; encrypt with an app key).
- **Sensitive data:** `medicalNotes`, `welfareNotes`, emergency contacts, guardian contacts, DOB, address on `PlayerProfile`. These are special-category and children's data. Field visibility (`lib/authz/field-visibility.ts`) gates them on the player endpoints, **but SEC-01 bypasses it completely**.
- **Encryption at rest:** provider-level only (Neon, R2). No column-level encryption for medical/welfare notes. Recommended given the sensitivity.
- **Logging:** `AuditLog` for registrations, attendance corrections, roster export, account export/delete, safeguarding updates. `LoginAttempt` stores SHA-256 of the IP (unsalted, so IPv4 is reversible by brute force; LOW). `console.error` of unknown errors with request id (Vercel logs).
- **Retention:** LoginAttempt pruned after 24 h (opportunistic). **No automated retention jobs** for the Privacy Policy's proposed schedule (6 y / 2 y / 25th birthday). The policy describes retention that the system does not enforce.
- **Deletion:** self-service anonymisation (`lib/account.ts#anonymiseAccount`), last admin protected. Guardians deleting a child's data: via club only.
- **Foreign keys:** cascades sensible (`PlayerProfile.user onDelete: Cascade`, registration team `SetNull`). Deleting a team (`DELETE /teams/[id]`) hard-deletes; check the cascade impact on memberships/events before use.
- **Tenant isolation:** single club. `clubId` scoping is partial (W2 follow-up, `Team.clubId` nullable). N/A today.
- **Backups:** Neon point-in-time restore depends on plan. **Not evidenced**, so requires verification.
- **Can users access other users' data?** **Yes, via SEC-01 (any user), SEC-04/05 (emails/DOB), SEC-02 (bucket objects given a key).**

## 10. UK Legal & Regulatory Review

- **UK GDPR / DPA 2018 (as amended by DUAA 2025).** The club processes children's data and special-category health data, so a high standard applies.
  - *Transparency:* the privacy notice exists and is detailed. It names processors (Vercel, Neon, R2/B2, Resend) and US transfers. But it is **published as a draft**, has **placeholder controller contact details and contact email** (Art. 13(1)(a) requires the controller's identity and contact details), and **omits Vercel Analytics/Speed Insights and push services**. The cookies section claims no analytics.
  - *Lawful basis:* stated per purpose (6(1)(b), (c)/(f), Art. 9 explicit consent for health). Reasonable; **requires verification** that the consent documents actually capture explicit consent for medical data from a guardian.
  - *Storage limitation:* retention schedule is "proposed", not adopted or enforced.
  - *Security (Art. 32):* **SEC-01 is a likely personal-data breach risk.** If it has been reachable in production by any signed-in member, the club must assess it under Art. 33 (72-hour ICO notification if risk to individuals is likely). With only a test team and a handful of accounts live, the actual exposure may be small. **Check access logs** (Vercel runtime logs for `GET /api/v1/videos/`).
  - *Accountability:* a DPIA appears necessary (children's data, special category, systematic monitoring of attendance). None is evidenced.
  - *DUAA 2025:* controllers must have a process for data-protection complaints (in force by June 2026). The policy mentions complaining "to us directly" but gives no channel. **Requires completion.**
  - *ICO fee:* Diverse Youth NI may be exempt (not-for-profit exemption is narrow) or already registered. **Requires verification**; show the ICO registration number in the notice if registered.
- **PECR (as amended by DUAA 2025; cookie changes in force 5 Feb 2026).** The site stores: the NextAuth session cookie (strictly necessary), the registration draft cookie (strictly necessary), `localStorage` theme/`nextauth.message` (functional/"appearance" exception now available), and the service worker. Vercel Web Analytics is cookieless, but it still collects data; whether it is "storage/access" depends on the script's behaviour. The DUAA statistical-purposes exception requires clear information **and an opt-out**, so at minimum **disclose analytics and offer an opt-out**. No cookie banner is required for the current set, provided disclosure is fixed. **Potential issue.**
- **Children's code (AADC).** Likely in scope: an online service likely to be accessed by under-18s. Good: guardian registration for minors, public profiles off by default and admin-approved, DM safeguarding policy, no ads/profiling. Gaps: no DPIA evidenced, no child-friendly privacy information (standard 4), analytics not disclosed (standard 3/8), over-exposure of minors' DOB/email to teammates (standard 7 "default settings"/data minimisation), and geolocation N/A.
- **Equality Act 2010.** As a service provider, the club must make reasonable adjustments. WCAG 2.2 AA is the usual benchmark. The public site has WCAG A failures (2.2.2). The authenticated app is untested. **No accessibility statement.** (The Public Sector Bodies Accessibility Regulations 2018 don't apply unless DYNI is publicly funded to the extent that it is a public sector body. **Requires verification.**)
- **Consumer protection / e-commerce.** No payments, so mostly N/A. If the club is a company or charity: the **Electronic Commerce (EC Directive) Regulations 2002 reg. 6** information duty (name, geographic address, email, registration/charity number) applies to "information society services". The site has **no address, email or registration numbers**. **Potential issue.** A Northern Ireland registered charity must also state its charity status/number on certain documents, including websites, under the Charities Act (Northern Ireland) 2008. **Requires verification.**
- **Copyright/IP.** Fonts are self-hosted (Archivo, Inter, Barlow Condensed, IBM Plex Mono — all OFL, so OK). The crest/logo is the club's own. No third-party photos yet (placeholders). No issue found.

### Detail — SEC-01 (CRITICAL)
**Attack:** a signed-in player (any role) requests `GET /api/v1/videos/1`, `/2`, … Each video assigned to a player returns that player's whole `PlayerProfile` row (DOB, address, emergency contacts, `medicalNotes`, `welfareNotes`, guardian contact) and their whole `User` row (`email`, bcrypt `passwordHash`, `calendarToken`, `mustChangePassword`). The calendar token alone grants that user's ICS feed (event locations and times for a child). Password hashes enable offline cracking, and the temporary passwords (≈21 bits) would fall quickly.
**Why it's still open:** commit `fb63fbf` (scoping fix) is on the local branch only, and even it keeps `include: { user: true }`.
**Fix:** see register. After deploying, review Vercel logs for unusual `/api/v1/videos/` access to decide on Art. 33 notification, and force-reset passwords of affected accounts if there is evidence of access.

A public site and members' platform for DYNI Blazers, a Belfast community basketball club run by Diverse Youth Northern Ireland. It handles registration (guardian-led for under-18s), scheduling, attendance, development, media, communication and safeguarding.
